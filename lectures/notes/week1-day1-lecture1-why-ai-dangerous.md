## Summary

AI gets automation done before the people needing to prescribe how it should be done beacuse otherwise it would not be AI. By doing so it will end up depeloping intergnal subgoals that are not prescribed and can be harmful.

**Harmful subgoals:** Even a harmless objective can leave harmful strategies available.

**Instrumental convergent subgoals:** There are all sort of subgoals that are generally usefull but generally harmful. For instance: money, political power, lying, weapons, etc. 
Not every instrumentally convergent goal is bad, for example cooperation.
