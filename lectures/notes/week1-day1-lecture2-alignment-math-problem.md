## Summary

**The alignment problem**:

What do we want the AI to do and how do we get it to do that?

> * *Coherent Extrapolated Volition:* What would humanity do?
> * *Intent alignment:* Try to get the AI to do what we want it to do.
> * *Constitution:* AI should align to an written document balancing guidelines, ethics, etc.
> * *Corrigibility:* Even if the AI is much smarther than humand it will still be open to correction. 


**How the alignment pipeline would look like?**

We can make a decomposition into outer alignment and inner alignment:

*Outer:* reward function should favour behaviour that satisties alignment target. A misspecified reward function can lead to unwanted behaviour scoring well. \
*Inner:* Once we got the reward function, what guarantees we have that with our training algoritm we are going to find this aligned policy? The failure mode of the inner alignment is goal misgeneralization where the model is trained on the reward favoring right behaviour but the model learns the wrong goal. The generalization step is a mistery.
