About

This page collects my personal notes, code, and resources from the SISSA School on the Mathematical Foundations of AI Safety, held at SISSA (Scuola Internazionale Superiore di Studi Avanzati), Trieste, Italy, November 16–27, 2026.

The school covers the mathematical and scientific underpinnings of AI alignment, including agent foundations, scalable oversight, singular learning theory, and formal verification with Lean. Week 1 focuses on the foundations of AI alignment; Week 2 on deep learning theory and singular learning theory.

Notes are written in Markdown with LaTeX mathematics and loaded live from the repository. Click any lecture below to expand its notes.
