About

This page collects my personal notes, code, and resources from the SISSA School on the Mathematical Foundations of AI Safety, held at SISSA (Scuola Internazionale Superiore di Studi Avanzati), Trieste, Italy, November 16–27, 2026.

The school covers the mathematical and scientific underpinnings of AI alignment, including agent foundations, scalable oversight, singular learning theory, and formal verification with Lean. The two-week programme begins with the foundations of AI alignment (Week 1) and transitions to deep learning theory and singular learning theory (Week 2).

Notes are written in Markdown with LaTeX for mathematics. Code and tutorials are in Jupyter notebooks. All material is provisional and will be updated as the school progresses.
